About the event

Ideas worth spreading, from our campus.

TEDxGGI brings students, thinkers and builders together at GGI Campus for a day of talks, stories and conversation. This year we're asking a deceptively simple question: what if the world you see is only the version you've agreed to?

Under the TEDx licence, we're a community-run event, curated by students, built on curiosity.

What is TEDx?

TEDx is a programme of local, self-organized events that bring people together to share a TED-like experience.

Who can attend?

Students, faculty and members of the public can attend once registration opens.

Will the talks be recorded?

Selected talks may be shared online after the event, subject to TEDx guidelines.
